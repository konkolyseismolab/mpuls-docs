struct ConvectiveParameters

Collaboration diagram for ConvectiveParameters:

Dimensionless parameters controlling convection and turbulence.

These parameters correspond to the α–coefficients used in time–dependent convection models (e.g. Kuhfuß, Stellingwerf, or similar 1D hydrodynamic formalisms). They scale the strength of mixing-length convection, convective flux, turbulent pressure, turbulent energy, and turbulent flux.

All parameters are dimensionless.
