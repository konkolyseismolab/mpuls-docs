struct EosState

Thermodynamic state returned by EOS routines.

Contains density, specific volume, gas pressure, and radiation pressure. All quantities are assumed to be in cgs units.

Public Members

Density ρ [g/cm³].

double specific_volume

Specific volume V = 1/ρ [cm³/g].

double gas_pressure

Gas pressure p_g [dyn/cm²].

double rad_pressure

Radiation pressure p_r [dyn/cm²].
